game: crossword
date: 2026-11-26

[Crossword](/crossword/) now has a new puzzle every day: a classic 15×15
newspaper-style grid with a light theme, where every letter is crossed.

Pick Easy, Medium or Hard. The grid is the same, but the clues change, and
Hard hides answer lengths and turns off autocheck. Each difficulty keeps its
own progress and time, and past puzzles are in the archive.

The Thanksgiving special is still there under Specials.
