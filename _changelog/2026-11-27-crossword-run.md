[Crossword Run](/crossword/run/) is a new, optional way to play: a roguelike
built for phones.

Climb three acts of small crosswords, from 5×5 minis up to an 11×11 final
boss. Pick your path through each act: puzzles, tougher elite grids with a
curse, shops, rest stops and odd encounters. Ink is your life. Finish a word
wrong and every wrong letter costs a drop, while correct words lock in.
Win puzzles to collect perks and tools for the climb.

There's an on-screen keyboard, your run saves after every move, and a daily
run that's the same for everyone, with a result you can share. The regular
daily crossword is unchanged.
