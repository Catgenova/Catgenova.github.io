---
title: Three different daily puzzles
game: crossword
date: 2026-11-27
---

The daily [Crossword](/crossword/) now gives you three different puzzles a
day instead of one grid at three clue levels:

- **Small**: a quick 7×7 with easy clues.
- **Medium**: an 11×11 with medium clues.
- **Large**: the themed 15×15 with hard clues.

No answer repeats across a day's three puzzles, so you can play them all.
Every past day in the archive has all three. If you'd started a daily
before, your progress is still there under that day's Large puzzle.
