A giant criss-cross crossword with 200 answers, all about Thanksgiving: the
dishes on the table, the 1620 voyage and the 1621 harvest feast, parades and
football, autumn, and the bird itself.

Clues are pitched at medium difficulty, and each one shows the answer's
length, with multi-word answers broken up like (5,4). Check or reveal a
square, a word or the whole grid if you get stuck, or turn on autocheck to
see mistakes as you type.

Your progress and time are saved in your browser, so you can close the tab
and pick up where you left off. It works with a keyboard on a computer and
with the on-screen keyboard on a phone.
