Every creature is assembled from a library of hand-drawn parts, each class on
its own skeleton: mammals, reptiles, fish, birds, insects, fungi, oozes and
more. Catch them in battle, raise them, and take any two of a class to the
shrine to fuse them into one that inherits from both.

Battles are turn-based with a party of five, and deep: hundreds of moves and
passives, weather and terrain, screens and hazards, status effects and
switching. It plays in the browser on a phone or a desktop, and your game is
saved on your device.
