Ride a mount from one edge of the level to the finish gate using a single
button. Each mount turns that button into a different move: a horse jumps, a
dragon pitches up while held, a griffin flaps, a war boar flips gravity, a
wisp flies diagonally, a siege cart charges its jump, and a shadow cat leaps
between floor and ceiling.

LyreFlyer is built around its in-game level editor, so every level can be
built, played and tested right away, with triggers, songs and camera moves
to make each one your own.
