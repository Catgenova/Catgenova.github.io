You don't control your heroes in a fight. Between raids you pick three of
them, forge their gear, spend their skill points, slot their spells and write
the tactics they fight by. Then you drop into a 30-minute raid you can steer
but not micromanage: hunt what lives there, carve what you kill, and reach an
extraction point.

Everything your heroes wear is at risk until they extract, and rival squads
are hunting the same ground. Carved parts go to the blacksmith, where the
species, the part and its grade decide what a piece of gear does. There are
no random affixes: everything you wear is something you went and got. Take a
solo monster for the first time and its trophy unlocks a new hero class.

Progress is saved on your device.
