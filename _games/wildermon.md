Wildermon is a multiplayer island game drawn in 2.5D isometric, played right
in your browser: one shared island, with its terrain and sprites generated
procedurally.

Set up an account with just a username and a password (no email address, and
nothing is ever sent to you) and your progress is kept on the island,
autosaving as you play.
