Quick entry

Type a whole phrase as text and press Enter. It is added after the cursor in the editor.

Quick entry syntax

6:3
String 6, fret 3. Strings count from 1 (the thinnest) to 6 (the thickest).
5:2+4:0
Several strings at once: join them with a plus sign.
3:5h7
Techniques: h hammer-on, p pull-off, b bend, r release, / slide up, \ slide down, ~ vibrato, x muted.
G, Am, F#m, D7
A chord name, starting with a capital letter, adds a full chord shape.
x32010
A chord written string 6 to 1. Use dashes for frets above 9: x-12-14-14-13-x.
w h q e s
Note lengths: whole, half, quarter, eighth, sixteenth. Add a dot for dotted, as in q. The length applies to everything after it.
|
Bar line.
-
A rest, with the current length.
Em*4
Repeat anything with a star and a count.
[Chorus]
Start a new section with a name.

Your tabs

Every tab is saved in this browser as you work. A backup file keeps them safe and moves them to another computer.

    Share your tab

    Three versions update as you write. The standard tab is for posting online or sending to sighted players. The spoken version lists each bar as a sentence for a screen reader. Braille music is for a braille display or embosser.

    Braille music

    Pitch and rhythm in braille music code, one octave above sounding pitch, the way guitar music is written. Lines are 40 cells. Titles and headings are in uncontracted Unified English Braille.

    The PDF and pictures show the dots for sighted teachers and transcribers. To emboss, use the BRF file.

    Read an existing tab

    Paste a text tab from a website or an email, or open a text file. It opens as a new tab in your list, where you can move through it note by note, hear it, and edit it.

    Editor keys

    Keys that work while the tab editor has focus
    KeyWhat it does
    Left RightPrevious or next column. Moving past the end adds a new column.
    Up DownMove toward string 1 (thinnest) or string 6 (thickest).
    Page Up Page DownPrevious or next bar. Control with Left or Right does the same.
    Home EndStart or end of the tab.
    0 to 9Enter a fret. Type two digits in a row for frets 10 and up.
    xMuted string.
    h p b r / \ ~Add a technique after the fret: hammer-on, pull-off, bend, release, slide up, slide down, vibrato. Then type the target fret, for example 5 h 7.
    [ ]Make this column's note shorter or longer: sixteenth, eighth, quarter, half, whole.
    .Dotted note on or off.
    lSay this column's note length and how full the bar is.
    EnterGo to the next column, adding one at the end if needed. New columns copy the current note length.
    BackspaceRemove the last character typed, or clear the note.
    Delete or -Clear the note on this string.
    Shift+Delete or Shift+BackspaceRemove the whole column, bar line or section label.
    iInsert an empty column before this one.
    aInsert an empty column after this one.
    dDuplicate this column, for repeated strums.
    m or |End the bar here: adds a bar line, says whether the bar is full, and moves into the next bar.
    SpacePlay this column.
    Shift+SpacePlay from here to the end. Escape stops.
    wWhere am I: bar, position, length, string and the whole column.
    Shift+WRead the whole bar.
    tRead the tuning and time signature.
    qJump to quick entry. Escape there comes back.
    Control+Z, Control+YUndo and redo. Command on a Mac.
    ?Hear a short key summary.

    NVDA and JAWS switch to focus mode in the editor on their own. With VoiceOver on a Mac, turn Quick Nav off (press Left and Right arrow together) so arrow keys reach the editor.

    Settings

    On a phone, the touch controls and quick entry replace the keyboard. On iPhone, sound plays even with the ring switch on silent in iOS 16.4 and later; on older versions, switch silent mode off to hear notes.
