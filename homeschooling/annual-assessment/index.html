Annual Assessment

Filed with the fourth quarterly report. Depending on grade, it's a written narrative or standardized test results. Upload the test's score report and it's attached to the same PDF.

Evaluator

A New York State certified teacher, a home instruction peer review panel, or another person the superintendent agrees to. Check with your district before a parent writes it.
