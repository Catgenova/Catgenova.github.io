Letter of Intent

Tells the superintendent you'll provide home instruction this school year. Due by July 1 every year, one per child, or within 14 days of starting home instruction mid-year.

Letter

Addressed to

The letter cites §100.10, includes the student's name, date of birth and grade, and asks the district for the IHIP form. It doesn't need to say why you're homeschooling.
