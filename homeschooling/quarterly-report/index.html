Quarterly Reports

Four a year per child, due on the dates in the IHIP. Each one gives the hours of instruction, what was covered in each subject, and a grade or evaluation, plus an explanation for any subject where less than 80% of the planned material was covered.

Period and hours

Subjects

List what was covered (units, chapters, books, projects), one per line. For the grade, use whatever you use consistently: a percentage, a letter, or Pass / Satisfactory.
