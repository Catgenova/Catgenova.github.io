Family setup

Enter these once. Every form fills them in, and they're saved in this browser as you type.

Parents and home

School district

Reports go to the superintendent of the public school district where you live (in New York City, the Central Office of Home Schooling).

School year

Default quarterly report due dates

Used when you start an IHIP. You can change them on each IHIP, and whatever the IHIP says is what the reports use.

Students

One letter of intent, IHIP, set of quarterly reports and annual assessment per student, every year. Set each grade for the current school year. Other years count up or down from it.

Back up and move

Your work lives in this browser only. Clearing site data or switching devices loses it unless you keep a backup file.
