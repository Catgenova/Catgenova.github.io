*Last updated: October 8, 2026*

This policy covers pere.games and the games hosted on it.

## What we collect

**This website.** The hub site doesn't ask for any personal information.
Like most websites, our host (GitHub Pages) and DNS provider (Cloudflare)
may log basic technical details such as your IP address and browser type to
keep the service running and secure.

**Game accounts.** Some of our games let you create an account so your
progress is saved online. Those games store your account details (such as
your email address or sign-in provider) and your game data with our database
provider, Supabase. We use this only to run the game. You can ask us to
delete your account and data at any time.

**Browser storage.** Our games may store settings and progress in your
browser's local storage. This stays on your device.

## Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. Google and its partners use
cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other
websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show
ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.

- You can opt out of personalized advertising in
  [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can learn more about how Google uses data at
  [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for
  personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before
personalized ads are shown.

## Children

Our games are meant for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has
given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.
